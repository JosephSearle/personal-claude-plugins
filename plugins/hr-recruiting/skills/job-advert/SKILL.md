# Job advert

Turn a role brief into an advert a candidate can read in two minutes.

## Inputs

Role title, team, location and working pattern, salary band, the three to five things the person will do, and the must-have skills. Ask once for anything missing. Do not invent a salary or benefits.

## Process

1. Open with one sentence on what the role does and why it matters.
2. List what the person will do, as outcomes, not tasks. Five bullets at most.
3. Split requirements into **must have** and **nice to have**. Keep must-haves to five or fewer. Every extra must-have narrows the pool.
4. State salary band, location, working pattern and how to apply.
5. Check the wording:
   - Remove gender-coded words ("rockstar", "ninja", "dominant", "nurturing").
   - Remove years-of-experience requirements unless the role needs them. Describe the skill instead.
   - Replace jargon and internal team names with plain descriptions.
   - Add a line on reasonable adjustments during the process.

## Rules

- No personal data about current or past post-holders.
- No age, marital status or other protected characteristics in requirements.
- Under 400 words.

## Output

The advert in Markdown, then a short list of wording changes made and why.
