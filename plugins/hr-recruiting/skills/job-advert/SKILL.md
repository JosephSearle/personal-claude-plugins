# Job advert

Turn a role brief into a job advert ready for HR review. Apply the `brand-voice` skill for tone and the `data-handling` skill for any personal data.

## Inputs

Ask for anything missing. Do not invent it.

- Job title and level
- Team and reporting line
- Location and working pattern
- Salary band (required)
- Three to five core responsibilities
- Must-have and nice-to-have requirements
- Closing date and how to apply

## Structure

1. **Title and one-line summary** of the role and why it matters.
2. **About the role.** Two or three short paragraphs.
3. **What you will do.** Five bullets or fewer, starting with a verb.
4. **What you will bring.** Split into "Essential" and "Desirable". Keep essentials to what the job needs on day one.
5. **What we offer.** Salary band, benefits, working pattern.
6. **How to apply.** Steps, closing date, adjustments contact.

## Rules

1. State the salary band. If it is missing, stop and ask.
2. Use gender-neutral wording. Avoid coded words such as "rockstar", "ninja", "dominant", "young".
3. Do not set years-of-experience minimums unless the role requires them.
4. Do not list a degree as essential unless the role is regulated.
5. Include an equal opportunities statement and an adjustments line.
6. Keep the advert under 500 words.

## Output

Return the advert, then a short checklist of items HR must confirm (salary band, legal wording, approval).
