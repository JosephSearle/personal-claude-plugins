# Campaign plan

Outline a campaign a team can review and cost. Apply the `brand-voice` skill for tone.

## Inputs

- Objective and the metric that defines success
- Audience segment
- Budget range and dates
- Product or offer

Ask for the objective, audience and dates if missing.

## Structure

1. **Objective.** One sentence with a target and a date.
2. **Audience.** Who they are, what they need, where they spend time.
3. **Message.** Core message and two supporting points.
4. **Channels.** For each: purpose, content type, owner, effort (low, medium, high).
5. **Timeline.** Weekly milestones from planning to review.
6. **Measures.** Leading indicators and the success metric. State how each is collected.
7. **Risks and dependencies.**

## Rules

1. Tie every channel to the objective. Drop channels that do not serve it.
2. Do not invent benchmark figures. Write "baseline needed" where none exists.
3. Do not commit budget the user has not stated.
4. Include a review point after launch.

## Output

The plan as a table of channels and a timeline, then the assumptions the team should confirm.
