# Campaign plan

Plan a campaign that can be measured.

## Inputs

The objective, the audience, the budget range, the launch date and the channels available. Ask for the objective and the date if missing.

## Process

1. **Objective** – one measurable target with a date.
2. **Audience** – primary and secondary segments.
3. **Message** – the core message and one proof point per segment.
4. **Channels** – each with its role in the campaign and its share of budget.
5. **Calendar** – a week-by-week table: channel, content piece, owner role, date.
6. **Measures** – leading and lagging metrics, and where each is reported.
7. **Risks** – top three, each with a fallback.

## Rules

- Every content piece in the calendar maps to one channel and one segment.
- Use `content-brief` for each new piece in the calendar.
- No personal data in audience definitions. Describe segments, not people.

## Output

The plan in Markdown, with the calendar as a table.
