# Content brief

Give a writer everything needed to draft the piece first time.

## Inputs

The idea, the content type, the target audience and the call to action. Ask for the audience and the call to action if missing. They decide everything else.

## Process

Fill these sections:

1. **Goal** – what the reader should think or do afterwards.
2. **Audience** – role, problem, and what they already know.
3. **Key message** – one sentence.
4. **Supporting points** – three, each with the evidence to use.
5. **Structure** – headings in order, with a word count per section.
6. **Call to action** – one.
7. **Search terms** – primary and two secondary, if the piece is for the web.
8. **Tone** – two or three words, plus one example sentence.

## Rules

- One key message per brief. Split the piece if there are two.
- No customer names or quotes without stated approval.
- Under 400 words.

## Output

The brief in Markdown.
