# Content brief

Give a writer everything needed to draft without a follow-up call. Apply the `brand-voice` skill for tone.

## Inputs

- Topic and working title
- Audience and the problem they have
- Goal: what the reader should think, feel or do
- Format and length
- Target keyword, if any

Ask for the audience and goal if missing. Propose the rest.

## Structure

1. **Working title** and two alternatives.
2. **Audience and goal.** One sentence each.
3. **Key message.** One sentence.
4. **Outline.** Headings with a one-line note on what each section covers.
5. **Evidence to include.** Data, examples or quotes the writer should find. Mark each as "needed" until sourced.
6. **Search intent and keywords.** Primary keyword, two or three related terms.
7. **Call to action.**
8. **Do not include.** Topics, claims or competitors to avoid.

## Rules

1. Do not invent statistics or quotes. Mark missing evidence as "needed".
2. One key message per piece.
3. Keep the brief to one page.

## Output

The brief, then three questions the writer should settle with the requester before starting.
