# Proposal draft

Draft a proposal the customer can read without a call. Apply the `brand-voice` skill for tone.

## Inputs

- Customer name and sector
- The problem in the customer's words
- Scope, timeline and commercial terms agreed so far
- Approved pricing (required)

Ask for any of these that are missing. Do not invent pricing, scope or dates.

## Structure

1. **Summary.** The problem, our proposal and the outcome, in five sentences or fewer.
2. **Your situation.** Reflect back what the customer told us.
3. **Our approach.** Phases or workstreams, each with the result it delivers.
4. **Timeline.** Milestones with dates.
5. **Investment.** Use only approved pricing. State what is and is not included.
6. **Why Acme.** Two or three points tied to the customer's needs. No unsupported superlatives.
7. **Next steps.** What happens in the next two weeks.

## Rules

1. Use the customer's terms for their problem.
2. Do not promise outcomes the scope does not support.
3. Do not state discounts, legal terms or guarantees unless the user supplied approved wording.
4. Flag items that need legal or finance review.

## Output

The draft, then a "before you send" checklist: pricing approved, legal terms reviewed, claims evidenced.
