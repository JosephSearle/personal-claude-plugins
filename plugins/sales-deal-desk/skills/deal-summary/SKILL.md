# Deal summary

Give a sales manager the whole deal in one page.

## Inputs

Call notes, emails, CRM fields or a verbal description of the opportunity. Work from what is given. Mark anything unknown as **Unknown** rather than guessing.

## Process

Fill these sections in order:

1. **Customer need** – the problem in the customer's words.
2. **Proposed solution** – what we sell them and why it fits.
3. **Value** – deal size, term, and the business outcome for the customer.
4. **Stakeholders** – roles only (economic buyer, champion, blocker). No personal contact details.
5. **Stage and close date** – with the evidence behind them.
6. **Risks** – top three, each with a mitigation.
7. **Next step** – one action, an owner role and a date.

## Rules

- Separate facts from assumptions. Prefix assumptions with "Assumed:".
- No customer pricing from other deals.
- One page. Cut detail before cutting a section.

## Output

The brief in Markdown, then a list of the unknowns that most affect the forecast.
